00

The shape, in one line

From a hero-collection game (buy pre-authored heroes) → to a single player-created character who awakens Talents to gain power. Creation is low-pressure and mostly cosmetic; your build — not a creation pick — defines you. A Path-of-Exile ethos on an action-RPG + base-building loop.

🧬You are one hero

No roster, no gacha shop. Everything you invest lands on the same character, so progression compounds instead of scattering.

✦Power = your build

Talents (awakened affinities), levels, weapon, skills, gear & shards — all grown over time, all re-specable.

♻️Nothing is locked

The only creation choice is soft: you grow past it. No "solve character creation" anxiety.

01

Creation — three steps, one real choice

Two picks are pure flavor; only the third touches mechanics — and even that is soft, because you grow past it.

Step 1 · cosmetic

Origin (1 of 5)

Human · Bio · Xeno · Mecha · Void. Zero mechanics — no stat lean, no gating. It themes your character skin and your whole base (walls, turrets, skybox), plus your seat in the lore coalition.

Step 2 · cosmetic

Gender (F / M)

A body/proportion swap on one shared, retargeted rig. Purely visual.

Step 3 · the build pick

First Talent

Pick any one element or Summon, free. The pick is which Talent — its tier is a bounded gamble, random D→S. Most land low; a lucky roll lands S.

Creation roll ≠ endgame roll

The First-Talent tier is capped at S — SS / SSS exist only through the full Awakening loop (§03). Creation never front-loads endgame power; the ceiling stays a goal you play toward. Pick Summon and you're bootstrapped with one weak D-tier starter creature, so the tamer path is playable from minute one.

02

Talents — the identity & power engine

A Talent is an awakened affinity. There are 11: ten elements across two independent rock-paper-scissors wheels, plus the elementless Summon meta-Talent.

Nature wheel — 7-cycle (each beats the next)
Earth Electric Water Fire Ice Wind Poison
Earth → Electric → Water → Fire → Ice → Wind → Poison → (Earth)
Celestial wheel — 3-cycle (each beats the next)
Light Dark Twilight ✦ Summon — not an element
Light → Dark → Twilight → (Light)  ·  the two wheels never cross-affinity

Every owned Talent has a tier

D→ C→ B→ A→ S→ SS→ SSS

The key mechanic — two layers of effect

Active / Present Talent
One at a time

The Talent you equip drives your identity icon, your global stats (its tier scales HP + Attack growth), and which battlefield Domain buff/debuff applies to you.

Swap freely — out of combat.

Owned Talents
All of them, always on

Your whole awakened library. Each element's skill power is boosted by that element's owned tier — regardless of what's currently active.

More Talents = a deeper spell library, not a bigger stat number.

Worked example

Active = Ice (Ice icon; Ice tier feeds your stats). You also own Fire SSS. You can still cast Fire skills — boosted by that owned Fire SSS. → "a Fire nuker wearing an Ice stat-suit." Global stats come from exactly one active Talent, so awakening more never balloons your raw numbers.

03

Awakening — the growth loop

The game's primary progression sink. You Awaken new Talents and Upgrade their tiers by spending three currencies. Breadth is cheap; height is the grind.

Awaken

Roll a new Talent's starting tier (weighted RNG). Fuelled by Awakening Stones — gameplay drops, so F2P can collect elements.

Upgrade

Raise an owned Talent one tier at a time — still RNG, with soft pity. Fuelled by Essence.

Reroll

Re-gamble a Talent's whole tier for Diamond — can drop as well as rise. The whale sink / accelerator.

① The Awaken gamble — tier-roll weights

TierDCBASSSSSS
Weight40%26%17%10%5%1.8%0.2%

S-or-better ≈ 7%. SSS ≈ 1-in-500 (a jackpot) — but a low roll is never dead, because Upgrade climbs there. The bounded creation roll drops SS/SSS and renormalizes to D 43 / C 28 / B 18 / A 8 / S 3%.

② Upgrade success — one tier per attempt

StepD→CC→BB→AA→SS→SSSS→SSS
Success75%55%38%22%11%4%

A failed attempt consumes materials, tier unchanged — but soft pity adds +5% per fail and resets on success, so high tiers grind instead of rage.

Design intent

Breadth is cheap — collect elements through play. Height is the money & grind — high tiers eat Essence and Reroll Diamond. There's no owned-count cap: eventually own all 11, because the endgame is tier, not count. The demand engine that makes you want breadth is Domains (§04).

04

Battlefield Domains — the tactical payoff

A battlefield can carry an element Domain: it empowers one element and suppresses another — reaching your side only (you + your summons; enemies are unaffected).

▲Empowered element

Your matching active Talent gains a stat package (layer 1), and your skills + summons of that element deal ~+30% (layer 2).

▼Suppressed element

Its stat package flips to a penalty, and that element's damage is cut ~−30%. Thematically the empowered element's opposite.

The tension it creates

Active Talent can't swap mid-fight, and Domains telegraph before you enter. So tonight's Water Domain forces a choice: run Water active (weak Water-tier stats, but the Domain buff) or your Fire SSS active (great stats, no buff — and a penalty if Fire is suppressed)? → The winning answer is to own a broad, high-tier portfolio and align per battlefield. That's why the Awakening economy has demand. The Base Scene even runs a rotating daily Domain as an optional live-ops ritual.

05

Leveling — XP you earn, never buy directly

Kills grant per-mob XP → XP accumulates → your level is derived from XP. The server validates every claim against the wave composition it owns, so per-mob granularity stays cheat-proof.

  • Level from XP. Smooth superlinear curve, hard max 150, banded 50-per-band. Only HP + Attack scale with level; the rest stay flat.
  • Band gate. XP earned past your unlocked cap is banked, not lost — it applies the instant the band-unlock gamble succeeds.
  • Diamonds buy XP books, not levels directly — the only diamond→level path.
  • Death isn't a fail state. Respawn is unlimited, gated by a per-respawn cooldown — the cooldown is the penalty, so XP accrues continuously.

07

The supporting axes

Talents are the engine; these are what you point it through.

⚔️Weapon

One axis replacing the old melee/ranged + physic/magic split — 30 weapons in 4 quadrants, freely swapped gear. Each encodes range, which defense it checks, and a signature twist.

Your equipped weapon filters which of your skills are live — swap Staff→Sword and a different set lights up. Same-family works; exact-type match gives +1 skill level.

📖Skills — unlocked by Talents, leveled by Seeds

Skills are unlocked through Talents by spending Skill Books — one Talent can unlock several. Your unlocked skills fill a preset of up to 10, laid out 5 left / 5 right on the command bar (keys 1–5 & Shift+1–5).

Seeds no longer grant skills. A socketed Seed now raises the level of every skill of its element — a Fire Seed levels all your Fire skills. So gear picks which elements you level; the preset picks which skills you press.

T — Empower (spends Soul), Space — Dash, and the 5 consumables stay separate — not part of the 10 slots.

🐾Summon pillar

The tamer path: capture → summon → grow → evolve a roster of persistent creatures. Their stats are character-independent (species × level × evolution × Summon tier), so investing in them genuinely matters.

Fielding costs Soul reservation (the same pool Empower uses — a wide army starves your burst) plus HP-drain upkeep. No hard cap: field as many as you can sustain.

💠Gear & Shards

10 gear slots (5 offense / 5 defense), each a rolled instance with a Seed socket. 10 element-colored Shard slots — one per element (Neutral retired, Twilight added).

Your hero uses all 10; captured creatures equip an element-locked subset from the same shared pool — so allocation is a real choice.
